From cotton to calm
How a Sakooon sheet is made
Follow the thread — every step is slow on purpose, so your sheet stays soft and strong for years.
01
The fibre
Long-staple cotton, picked for softness
Longer fibres spin into finer, stronger yarn — fewer loose ends, less pilling and a naturally smoother feel.
02
The weave
Woven tight for a silky drape
Percale or sateen, each thread count is woven to a precise density so it feels exactly as it should.
03
The finish
Pre-washed, soft from night one
Every piece is washed and finished before it reaches you — no stiffness, minimal shrinkage, true colour.
04
To your door
Folded, wrapped and gift-ready
Packed in a reusable fabric pouch and delivered across Pakistan — ready for bed the same night.
