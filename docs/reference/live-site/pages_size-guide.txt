01Which sheet size fits my mattress?
Measure your mattress width, length and depth. Our fitted sheets suit mattresses up to 12 inches deep. See the size guide for exact measurements.
02What does T200, T250 and T300 mean?
It is the thread count: threads woven into one square inch. Higher counts feel smoother and silkier; T200 is crisp and breathable, T300 is our softest sateen.
03How should I wash my sheets?
Wash cool (30–40°C) with mild detergent, tumble dry low and iron warm. Avoid bleach to keep colours rich.
04How long does delivery take?
2–3 working days in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, 3–5 days elsewhere. Cash on delivery is available nationwide.
05Can I exchange a product?
Yes, unused products in original packing can be exchanged within 7 days.
